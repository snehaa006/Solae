Solae

aesthetic, professional, and a little bit sandy.

The foundations for SOLAE's portfolio and store. The structure comes from k2o (warm cream, rounded cards, pill controls, a wide display face). Body type is SKIMS' Inter. The look comes from the pin boards: an editorial red serif on grainy cream paper (the late checkout), hand-cut wavy photo frames (SUIT), arches and waves (surf shop) and big spaced type over the sea (sea & soil).

01

brand

The wordmark is a tall, condensed high-contrast serif in Solae Red, taken from the brand sheet. The sun-streak is our answer to k2o's shooting star: a tapering line that ends in a little sun. Use it as a divider, after headings, and as a sign-off.

rules

wordmarkAlways set in caps, tracked out at .12em and stretched vertically. Solae Red on light backgrounds, Coral on Deep Teal. Never inside a heading.
tagline"by teens, for teens" in Instrument Serif italic as a script overlay, or in tracked caps under the wordmark.
caseHeadlines and buttons are lowercase (k2o, late checkout). Section labels (wide display), nav and eyebrows are tracked caps.

02

colour

Every hex comes from the brand sheet's two colour drafts. Sand is the page; Deep Teal is the ink. Brights are for illustration, stripes and big moments. Small text only ever uses ink, stormy or brick. The contrast labels below are worked out live in the page.

--stripe-cabana: from the brand sheet's stripe page. Use it for footers, progress bars, product tiles and the mobile menu.

03

type

Six roles, each with one job. k2o's Moslin and TT Turns are commercial fonts you have to license, so free Google stand-ins are in place for now. The real names come first in every font stack, so once you license and self-host a font, it takes over automatically. Inter is SKIMS' actual body font, and it's free.

08

voice

We sound like friends who've been to the beach, not a catalogue. Lowercase, warm, specific. Always say what something does (pads that stay put) rather than how it feels.

say

knee-length, so they never ride up when you climb out of the pool.

not

Experience unparalleled comfort with our premium performance shorts.

say

the white one goes with everything.
