aesthetic, professional, and a little bit sandy.

The foundations for SOLAE's portfolio and store. Colours and logos come from the Brand Starter (28 October 2026). The structure comes from k2o (warm cream, rounded cards, pill controls, spaced caps). Body type is SKIMS' Inter. The look comes from the pin boards: an editorial deep teal serif on grainy cream paper (the late checkout), hand-cut wavy photo frames (SUIT), arches and waves (surf shop) and big spaced type over the sea (sea & soil).

rules

colourOptions 1 and 3 always keep their own colours, on white, sand or Deep Teal. Option 2's letters are Deep Teal on light and Sand on Deep Teal; its sun is always Sunshine.
sizeOption 1 needs room: below about 8rem wide the letters get hard to read, so in the header it's at its limit. Option 2 works from 1rem caps. Option 3's symbol works down to 16px, which is why it's the tab icon.
spaceLeave at least one sun's width of empty space around every logo. Option 1's flat edge can sit right on the edge of a page, card or box: the sun is rising out of it.
tagline"made for the sun", in the headline serif or as a handwritten note.
caseHeadlines and buttons are lowercase (k2o, late checkout). Section labels (spaced caps), nav and eyebrows are tracked caps, like the wordmark.

02

colour

Every hex comes from the Brand Starter: three main colours, a neutral and a few accents. Cream and Sand are the page, Deep Teal is the ink, Sunshine and Lagoon are the brights. The collection colours are for the swimsuits only. Small text only ever uses deep teal or stormy teal. The contrast labels below are worked out live in the page.

how much of each

About 60% sand or white, 25% deep teal, 10% sunshine and lagoon, 5% accents.

the cabana stripe

--stripe-cabana: the beach-towel stripe, in the brand colours. Kept for packaging and print; the site itself no longer uses it.

08

voice

We sound like friends who've been to the beach, not a catalogue. Lowercase, warm, specific. Always say what something does (pads that stay put) rather than how it feels.

say

knee-length, so they never ride up when you climb out of the pool.

not

Experience unparalleled comfort with our premium performance shorts.

say

the white one goes with everything.
