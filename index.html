our story

it started with a sketch of the sun

solae began with a sketch of the sun i drew in 2024, long before i knew how to make a swimsuit.

growing up in saudi arabia, i struggled to find swimwear that felt right for me. most options were either fully covered or not designed with modesty in mind, and very few were both stylish and comfortable. every summer, i found myself pairing a one-piece with mismatched shorts and wishing there was something better.

so i decided to create it. solae makes matching swim sets, a one-piece paired with shorts, for girls like me who want coverage, comfort and confidence without giving up on style.

the name comes from soleil, the french word for sun. to me, it represents what summer should feel like: bright, free and completely your own.

three things we care about

modest by design

coverage where you want it, without giving up on style. every suit is double-lined and fully opaque when wet, so it stays modest in the water and out of it.

tested in the water

if a suit slips when you dive, rides up when you climb out, or goes see-through when it's wet, it doesn't make the drop. simple.

made to match

every one-piece has shorts to match, knee-length or just above. cover-ups come in every suit colour too, plus white and black for the days you want it simple.
